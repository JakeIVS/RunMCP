Turn your AI agent into your personal coach

RunMCP is the all-in-one running coach MCP workspace for your AI agent. It keeps your goals, workouts, and training schedule all in one place.

See connection options How it works ↓

A coach with context, not a black box.

  1. 01 / Connect your agentAuthorize your preferred AI agent to use your RunMCP account.
  2. 02 / Set goals and intensityChoose conservative, balanced, or ambitious, then save the facts your agent needs.
  3. 03 / Review a proposed calendarYour agent reasons about training and submits a clear plan or patch with a rationale.
  4. 04 / Talk through real lifeLog runs, illness, time off, and other activities with your agent. It proposes the next revision explicitly.

Every change has a path.

Your agent brings the coaching judgment. RunMCP validates ownership and versions, then stores the agreed calendar and its history.
